Website Architecture: Exclusively for Admin & Super-Admin Maintenance

To ensure optimal user conversion and eliminate server hosting bloat in the MVP, the website does NOT serve consumer food ordering. Customers, Vendors, and Delivery Riders interact exclusively via the Unified Android Mobile App. The public website acts purely as the Super-Admin and Admin Operations Control Center for catalog moderation, store approvals, system health monitoring, and dispute resolution.

Multi-Tier Refund Management & Dispute Arbitration Engine

Transparent, three-window workflow ensuring customer trust while protecting restaurant partners against fraudulent claims.

Step 1 • In Mobile App

Customer Refund Request Window

Available inside the customer's Order History within 24 hours of delivery. Allows customers to raise formal claims with complete transparency.

  • Select issue category: Missing Dish, Spilled/Damaged, Wrong Item, or Extreme Delay
  • Upload real-time photo proof of the package/food
  • Enter customer refund UPI ID / Bank account details
  • Instant ticket generation with live tracking of refund status
Step 2 • In Mobile App

Vendor Claim Review Window

Kitchen operators receive an instant alert to review the customer's claim before any monetary deduction occurs.

  • Review customer claim description and uploaded photos
  • 1-Tap "Accept & Issue Full/Partial Refund" (deducted from vendor settlement)
  • 1-Tap "Contest Claim" with mandatory kitchen dispatch notes or CCTV reference
  • 24-Hour resolution timer before automatic admin escalation
Step 3 • On Web Portal

Super-Admin Arbitration Window

Central dispute desk on the MERN Web Admin console with final decision-making power.

  • Arbitrate contested claims between customer and restaurant
  • Override decisions with 1-Tap "Approve Platform Courtesy Refund" or "Approve Vendor-Chargeable Refund"
  • Automatic ledger reconciliation updating vendor payout sheets
  • Full audit trail of dispute resolutions stored in MongoDB
