Core Roles in the Unified Ecosystem

How all stakeholders interact through the single Android application and central MERN web console.

In Single Android App

1. Customer Experience

Target: Android Mobile Screen

Smooth ordering flow designed for everyday food delivery consumers.

  • Phone number OTP registration & profile with saved delivery addresses
  • Local restaurant discovery filtered by single-city radius
  • Menu categories, veg/non-veg tags, item variants, and add-on selection
  • Shopping cart with itemized pricing, taxes, delivery charges, and coupon discounts
  • Online payment (Razorpay UPI/Cards) & Cash on Delivery (COD)
  • 4-Stage Order Status Tracker: Placed ➔ Cooking ➔ Out for Delivery ➔ Delivered
  • 1-Tap "Call Delivery Boy" and 1-Tap "WhatsApp Support" buttons
In Single Android App

2. Vendor (Restaurant) Portal

Target: Android Mobile / Tablet Screen

Minimalist kitchen display for busy restaurant operators to manage food orders.

  • Loud incoming audio sound alert when a customer places an order
  • 1-Tap "Accept Order" (with estimated prep timer) or "Reject Order"
  • 1-Tap "Food Prepared & Ready for Pickup" to alert assigned rider
  • Quick menu stock manager: toggle dishes "In Stock" or "Out of Stock"
  • Daily completed orders count and total sales summary
In Single Android App

3. Delivery Partner View

Target: Android Mobile Screen

Battery-friendly interface for motorcycle and bicycle delivery boys.

  • Online / Offline duty switch to receive new delivery orders
  • Incoming delivery alert with restaurant pickup & customer delivery address
  • 1-Tap External Navigation: Launches native Google Maps app with pre-filled destination
  • Order pickup confirmation from kitchen
  • 4-Digit OTP verification with customer upon delivery handover
  • Daily delivery earnings counter
MERN Web Portal

4. Super Admin & Admin Website

Target: Desktop Web (React.js)

Centralized operations and governance dashboard for platform owners.

  • Secure Super Admin and Admin login with Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • Real-time live order monitoring queue with manual rider assignment fallback
  • Restaurant onboarding, KYC review, and menu item approval
  • Delivery partner registration and document verification
  • Platform commission rate configuration (% take-rate or flat delivery fee)
  • Coupon code creation and promotional banner management
  • Sales reports, cancellation records, and downloadable vendor payout sheets
