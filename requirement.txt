1. Project Overview
Web Miracle Technology proposes to develop a custom multi-vendor food delivery marketplace inspired by
Swiggy and Zomato. The platform will connect customers, restaurants, delivery partners, and administrators through
an integrated online ordering and delivery management system.

online payments, delivery assignment, order tracking, commission management, offers, customer support, and
business reporting.
The solution will include a customer-facing website, customer mobile application, restaurant management panel,
delivery partner mobile application, and a centralized admin dashboard, subject to the agreed platform scope.

6. Order Processing and Delivery Workflow
The platform will support the following end-to-end order lifecycle:
1. The customer selects a delivery address and browses available restaurants.
2. The customer selects food items and proceeds to checkout.
3. The system calculates the order amount, applicable charges, and discounts.
4. The customer places the order and completes payment or selects cash on delivery.
5. The restaurant receives the order and accepts or rejects it.
6. The restaurant updates the preparation status.
7. A delivery partner is assigned manually by the admin or through the agreed assignment workflow.
8. The delivery partner collects the order and updates the pickup status.
9. The customer receives order status updates and available tracking information.
10.The delivery partner completes delivery using the configured confirmation method.
11.The system records the completed order, payment status, commissions, and relevant reports.
Cancellation, refund, restaurant commission, and delivery fee rules will be configurable according to the approved
business requirements.
